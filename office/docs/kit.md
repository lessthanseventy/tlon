# office/kit/ — what every surface shares

`kit/` — what every office surface shares: the snapshot's types (`types.ts`, the shape of
`Server.Office.status`, served at `GET /api/office`), the data views (`crew.ts`: a workspace's view, the crew, the
whiteboard's columns), sprites and looks (`sprites.ts`), the parts library (`parts.ts`: slots, dials, `rollLook`), the colour roles (`palette.ts`), the
1x canvas and the **frame** a room hands a surface (`canvas.ts`; its speech balloons are 34×4 with long words hard-broken and an overflow ending `... (v)`, `v` being the key to the conversation, placed by `balloon.ts` so none leaves the viewport or overlaps another), tlon's own bitmap font in two cuts (`font.ts`), the
office's life (`sim.ts`: who walks where, Nina's day — a room supplies its geometry as a `Plan`; a channel down draws the room from the last good snapshot (`Sim.held`: furniture and people stay, the exec desks' plates go blank), dimmed — an outage is never the fire drill; the play card's fire drill row (`play("drill")`) raises a `DRILL`-long one: while `Sim.alarmed`, `closet()` in `rooms/wide.ts` burns, and for the first `PANIC` ticks the crew runs about the room arms up, one scream every few seconds, Nina on the zoomies and Argos with them; then they muster at `plan.muster` with coffee and Argos barks at the smoke), and the toy's other happenings — `kit/events.ts`: the `EVENTS` table of scripted scenes (drill, zoo, meteor, power cut, duck, Gary, a paper-plane storm that Argos waits under, a pizza party that pulls the idle crew to the kitchen and greases the whiteboard, a power nap that dims the floor, settles the idle on the couch and puts Nina on a keyboard) and the pure `propsAt` each draws; `Sim.event(kind?)` runs one at a time, tidies itself, touches no thread or turn, and calls `Sim.onEvent` for step 7; row 1 of the sandbox's play card plays one,
the floor's light as a function of the clock (`daylight.ts`: darkness, lamps lit, `dark`: the pets' bedtime and your pyjamas; lamps register on the `Scene` as they are drawn),
a pet's temperament (`temperament.ts`: warmth/wits/energy, -2..2, as policy over the sim's chance tables; `pets.ts` resolves `pets.json` — `TLON_PETS`; the cat slot is a cat, rabbit or bird, polled live like `looks.json`, absent means today's room byte for byte),
a coworker's soul (`souls.ts`: `~/.config/tlon/souls/<name>.md` — `TLON_SOULS` — parsed by `## ` headings into `sections`, forgiving; polled live by dir signature like `looks.json`, read from disk by `tui/souls.ts` (the kit's half is pure); read-only on the person's card; the server writes first drafts (`Server.Soul`, `tlon-cli soul`, never over an existing file) and the People section is read by `kit/company.ts` (`affinities`, `relation`) and `kit/sim.ts` for rivalries and who covers for whom, gated by the `souls` dial; a soul never decides work),
what Nina says (`voices.ts`, sweet or sassy by warmth; Argos, the wide room's dog, has his lines in `kit/pets.ts`; each morning after seven he fetches the paper once, `WideRoom.stepPaper`) — the fallback only: the server's model lines come first (`Sim.hear`, `Server.Office.Pets`), and the pair's exchanges (`duo`) are said turn by turn (`WideRoom.talkOut`),
a coworker's voice (`seat-voices.ts`: the toy design §2 table — canned banter per seat, the fallback `Sim.onTogether` uses when the server wrote nothing, in `--sandbox` always; `at`: a line that suits the place the pair are at, half the time, the plain builder's when the seat has none; `sore`: what the loser says when two rivals (`kit/company.ts`) break up a foosball or pool game, which `Sim` runs `bloodTicks` longer than any other), and Scharlach the sheriff (`scharlach.ts`: his nameplate, his mug, and a demerit ledger that ticks when a coworker's build goes red — presentation only, on the client, fed by the sheriff's workspace's triage),
two coworkers the room sees together — at a game, on the couch, at the coffee machine, passing in the hall — are handed to `Sim.onTogether`, and the TUI asks the server what they say (`POST /api/office/talk/:ws`, one model call per conversation, throttled there),
drawing people and Nina into a `Scene` (`draw.ts`: what each worker is doing mid-turn, a worker
making a fuss of a pet), the furniture every room has (`furniture.ts`),
the home's tiles as pixel art (`homeart.ts`: `TILE_ART`, kind → painter, a missing kind draws plain; `renderHome` the build grid as a Frame, `paintFloor` the placed tiles at their absolute cells; the snapshot's weather rains and snows on the garden tile),
the TV's channels (`tv.ts`: the desktop backdrop's ambient shows, retuned for a small screen, and
an aquarium),
and Uqbar's volume (`uqbar.ts`: sprites, flight, the lounge shelf's extra spine; `viewOf` lifts agent `uqbar` out of the roster into `Agents.uqbar`, so it never gets a desk or a crew row), and its posts as paper aeroplanes (`plane.ts`: pure flight, `freshPosts` off the activity feed, `handoffFrom` off the activity feed's `handoff_opened` rows; `WideRoom.fly` sends one to the thread lead's desk, else its card, and says whether Argos caught it: the TUI earmarks that post, keeps the newest `EARMARKS_MAX` in its state dir (`office-earmarks`), and the reader draws 🐾 on its header). The small joys are the room's own, deterministic: a reader settled in the nook for `STRAIGHTEN` ticks stands the knocked spine back up until the next session wakes; Nina swats a book flying low over her (`swatted`), once per pass, and it snaps shut for `FLINCH` ticks. Its idle life is `volume.ts`: while its session is awake and not mid-turn it takes up one errand at a time (`pickErrand`: reading over a typist's shoulder, the corkboard, tidying the shelf, veering from the bathroom mirror, baiting Nina, reading to Argos, the window, the TV, the hallway, a hat for Argos, looking over a hire made in the last five minutes, a nap on your desk after dark), lingers `DWELL`, says its line (`UQBAR`, through `Sim.line`, so the model may write it some) and rests `REST`; a new hour sends it to the clock, a landing (`noticed` "shipped") flies a lap, and a rise in the triage count is an erratum (ribbon in `failing`). Its tip names the errand (`doing`). No session, no errand and no line: the golden frames hold.
