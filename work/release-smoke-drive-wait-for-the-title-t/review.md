The fix is sound and surgical. `scripts/release-smoke.sh` inserts `wait:$title` before `/` and `wait:#$tid  $title` after Enter, both gated on the candidate's `office-drive.sh` supporting `wait:*` (the same gate the existing `loaded` wait uses). When the drive lacks `wait:*` the arrays stay empty and the call degenerates to the old behaviour — backward compatible.
