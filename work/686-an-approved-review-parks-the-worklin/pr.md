# #686: an approved review parks the workline itself, no stall

Closes #686.

Since c716f69 the review verdict ends the reviewer's session first (`Server.Staffing.end_review/1`). `submit_review` then answered `"next" => "call advance_stage"` to a session that no longer existed, so an approved workline sat at review with `awaiting nil` until the stalled-for-1h need reached the operator (#388, #393–#397).

Now the approve branch of `record_verdict` calls `advance/2` itself when no QA is owed: the thread parks on the operator, or queues under a standing approval or auto-land. `{:awaiting, t}` is reported as `{:ok, t}`, the verdict's contract. An advance refused there (review.md not committed, the queue down) is the very stall this fixes, so it is never swallowed: the refusal is posted on the thread and returned as `{:error, {:not_parked, why}}`, the verdict itself standing. `submit_review` builds its `next` line from the thread the verdict returned — awaiting whom, handed to QA, queued under a standing approval — or says the park was refused (`"parked" => false`), never a fixed string. `end_review` stays where it was (b91dcb9's law), `graded/2` is unchanged, `request_changes` is untouched. The review brief and the review continuation no longer tell the reviewer to call `advance_stage`; the brief's exit verb at review is `submit_review`.

Checked, test-first: `workline_test` (an approve verdict leaves the thread awaiting the operator; under a standing approval, an already graded one is queued; a park the advance refuses is said on the thread and returned not parked) and `workline_review_head_test` (`SubmitReview.execute` on a real repo parks it and its `next` names who it awaits; `SubmitReview.next/2` for each outcome; an approval that still stands queues a fresh verdict after a docs-only commit without the operator — park/3's branch the verdict now reaches). Each failed before its change and passes after; the approval-stands test pins a path that already worked. Seven older tests recorded a verdict on a repo-less thread and advanced by hand, kept green only by the swallow: they now say the park was refused (`reviewed!`). workline, review_head, qa, fresh-session, landing, ledger, send_back, maintain, operator-review and continuation suites: 227 pass. The stage-discipline steering eval gated `advance_stage` into the review brief; its review clause now wants `submit_review` and no `advance_stage` (`mix server.eval --deterministic`: 11 passed, 0 failed). `server:check` is run by the service on the branch head; its verdict is posted on the thread.
